## [Understanding AI Harnesses](https://earendil.com/posts/what-is-a-harness/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

An AI harness is a software infrastructure that enables large language models \(LLMs\) to operate as agents, managing tool use, memory, and state persistence. It is crucial for developing and managing AI agents, as it bridges the gap between LLM capabilities and practical application, impacting how these models are integrated into real-world systems. A harness typically includes components like a command-line interface \(CLI\) for interaction, skill management, and orchestration, though it may have limitations in flexibility and customization.

**Background**: In AI/ML, a harness acts as a framework around LLMs, allowing them to perform tasks beyond simple prompting by integrating tools and managing complex workflows.
