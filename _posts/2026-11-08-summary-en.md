## [Commodore PET: The First Personal Computer](https://www.commodore.ca/commodore-products/commodore-pet-the-worlds-first-personal-computer/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Commodore PET, introduced in 1977, is recognized as one of the first personal computers, featuring an all-in-one design with a MOS Technology 6502 microprocessor and Commodore BASIC. Its historical significance lies in popularizing personal computing, influencing early industry standards, and its legacy seen in modern uses like ASCII art fonts. The PET 2001 model included a cassette deck and a small keyboard, with later models offering improved memory and displays. It was widely used in education and business.
