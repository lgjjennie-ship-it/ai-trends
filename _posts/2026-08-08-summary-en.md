## [New all-sky map reveals half a million supermassive black holes](https://www.sdss.org/black-hole-mapper-release-20/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A new all-sky map has been released, identifying half a million supermassive black holes and doubling the known X-ray sources. This groundbreaking map significantly expands our understanding of the universe and could lead to new discoveries in astrophysics and cosmology. The map was created using data from the SDSS and eROSITA X-ray surveys, providing unprecedented detail on the distribution of supermassive black holes.

This initiative is significant as it could impact the AI/ML field by promoting open-weight models, fostering innovation, and ensuring transparency. The initiative will focus on creating models that are publicly accessible and can be modified, with the first model, Genesis-Science-1, already unveiled.
