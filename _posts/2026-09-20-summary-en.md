ZK-JPEG is a new technology that enables zero-knowledge image editing and compression, allowing users to verify the authenticity of images without revealing the editing details. This technology is significant because it enhances data privacy and security in image editing, which could impact industries like digital media, forensics, and AI-driven content creation. ZK-JPEG integrates zero-knowledge proofs into JPEG compression and can verify a wide range of image transformations, such as merging layers and applying watermarks, while keeping certain details secret.

**Background**: Zero-knowledge proofs in cryptography allow one party to prove the validity of a statement to another without revealing the underlying information. JPEG is a widely used standard for image compression.
