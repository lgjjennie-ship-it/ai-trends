## [DeepMind&\#x27;s AI model boosts cyclone forecasting](https://deepmind.google/blog/weathernext-ai-model-achieves-breakthrough-in-forecasting-cyclones/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

DeepMind&\#x27;s WeatherNext model achieves a breakthrough in forecasting cyclones using advanced AI techniques, providing an extra day of warning. This advancement is significant as it enhances the accuracy of cyclone predictions, which can save lives and reduce economic damage, impacting global weather forecasting practices. The model uses multi-scale Graph Neural Networks, which are efficient and outperform traditional Numerical Weather Prediction models in terms of accuracy and speed.
